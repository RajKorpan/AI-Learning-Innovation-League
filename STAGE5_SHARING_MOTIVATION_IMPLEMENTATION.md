# Stage 5 — Sharing and motivation implementation

## 57. Keep Share flexible — IMPLEMENTED
- Share Builder begins by asking what kind of progress the team wants to center.
- Five valid share focuses are explicit: what was learned; problem identified; early prototype; test/revision; more developed tool.
- Students are explicitly told that a polished final product is not required.
- The show step permits learning clues, task maps, early prototypes, test evidence, revisions, or a tool interaction.
- Testing questions allow teams to state that testing has not happened yet and identify the next test.
- Facilitator Session 5 is aligned to the same progress-not-polish framing.

## 58. General showcase language — IMPLEMENTED
- No fixed event date or required venue appears in the public site.
- Share stage explains that sharing may be a Learning Showcase, club share-out, classroom conversation, gallery walk, peer review, demonstration, or another approved format.
- Exact venue/date are explicitly handled separately from the core website.
- “Before the Learning Showcase” was replaced on the student checkpoint with “Before you share.”

## 59. Sharing as motivation from the beginning — IMPLEMENTED
- Students landing page now contains a prominent “Where this leads” callout directly in the opening experience.
- The Share journey card describes the range of progress students may eventually share.
- Empathize Step 1 now tells students that early clues can become part of their later story.
- Home page description reinforces that teams share what they made and learned, including developing work.
