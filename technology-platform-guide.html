Technology and Platform Guide

Choose tools that fit your learners, school, and project.

The best platform is not necessarily the most powerful. It is the one that supports the learning goal while meeting school, privacy, age, accessibility, and cost requirements.

Platform selection

Questions to ask before choosing

Access

Do students have compatible devices, internet access, accounts, and permission to use the tool?

Privacy

What data are collected, retained, shared, or used for model improvement?

Control

Can students define the role, feedback, constraints, and interaction sequence?

Accessibility

Does the platform work with keyboards, screen readers, captions, zoom, and alternative input methods?

Cost

Is the free version sufficient? Are paid features required for sharing or saving work?

School compatibility

Is the site blocked, restricted by age, or inconsistent with local policy?

Prototype options

No-code AI tool

Best when students can safely configure prompts, rules, and interactions without programming.

General AI assistant

Useful for testing structured prompts when the tool is school-approved and privacy requirements are met.

Paper or storyboard prototype

Best when student accounts are unavailable, tools are blocked, or the team needs to test the learning design first.

Advisor checklist

Document the decision

  • Platform name and purpose
  • Required student age and account type
  • Data collected and retained
  • Cost and device requirements
  • Accessibility strengths and limitations
  • School approval status
  • Backup option if access fails
Platform review record

Recommended documentation

AreaQuestions
AccountsWho creates the account? What age restrictions apply? Can students use aliases or teacher-managed access?
DataWhat prompts, files, conversations, or identifiers are stored?
SharingCan projects be made public? Can access be limited?
ControlCan the team specify system instructions, refuse certain requests, and review transcripts?
AccessibilityAre keyboard use, screen readers, captions, and readable contrast supported?
FallbackWhat happens if the platform is blocked, unavailable, or inappropriate?
