Assessment and Feedback

Collect evidence about learning, usability, and implementation.

Use multiple forms of evidence rather than relying only on student satisfaction or a functioning prototype.

Student pre/post questionnaire

Human-learning knowledge, AI-literacy knowledge, responsible use and privacy, metacognition, attitudes, and confidence identifying a challenge.

Performance task

Students identify a challenge, choose a strategy, describe an AI interaction, and name a safeguard.

Project rubric

Evaluate goal definition, diagnosis, task analysis, strategy, AI design, safety, testing, revision, and communication.

Teacher survey

Clarity, age appropriateness, feasibility, time, engagement, access, policy, accessibility, reuse, and revisions.

Advisor usability test

Can advisors find lessons, safety guidance, startup instructions, tool-building supports, group management, and expected outcomes?

Family and student feedback

Transparency, perceived value, accessibility, student support, engagement, and community relevance.

Recommended evidence package

  • Pre/post student scores
  • Student performance-task results
  • Project rubric scores
  • Teacher and advisor feedback
  • Usability task completion
  • Student products and revision logs
  • Implementation observations
  • De-identified family feedback
