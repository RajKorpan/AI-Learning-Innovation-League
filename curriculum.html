Five-Lesson Curriculum

Build shared knowledge before students build tools.

The curriculum combines learning science, AI literacy, task diagnosis, responsible design, and iterative testing. Each lesson produces an artifact that becomes part of the final student project.

Curriculum at a glance

Five lessons, one connected design journey

1

Humans Learn; AI Generates

Students compare human learning, prior knowledge, practice, understanding, and expertise with the pattern-based outputs produced by generative AI.
2

Diagnose the Learning Challenge

Students move from a broad difficulty to a specific, evidence-supported challenge using interviews, observation, work samples, and task analysis.
3

Match the Challenge to a Strategy

Students compare retrieval, elaboration, worked examples, feedback, chunking, self-explanation, and metacognitive reflection.
4

Design an AI-Supported Learning Aid

Students define the AI role, audience, interaction sequence, feedback rules, limits, safeguards, and learner responsibilities.
5

Test, Evaluate, Revise, and Share

Students test the prototype, evaluate accuracy and usability, revise from evidence, and explain limitations.

Recommended lesson structure

Use a consistent routine

Essential question

A student-friendly question that frames the lesson.

Core concepts

Learning-science and AI-literacy ideas in clear language.

Active task

A sort, interview, model, design activity, or user test.

Student artifact

A saved project component that supports the next lesson.

Advisor guidance

Preparation, timing, misconceptions, questions, and adaptations.

Assessment check

An exit ticket, reflection, observation, or rubric checkpoint.
