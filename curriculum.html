Five-Lesson Curriculum

A compact foundation for the human-centered design cycle.

Each lesson introduces core ideas and produces an artifact for the shared team product. Advisors may adapt timing and examples without changing the central learning goals.

Lesson 1

Understand Human Learning and AI

Productive struggle, metacognition, self-regulation, human-versus-machine reasoning, AI roles, and learning myths.
Lesson 2

Listen Through Empathy Interviews

Teacher-client and peer learner conversations, stories, follow-up questions, permission, notes, and synthesis.
Lesson 3

Define and Research the Problem

Problem size, task analysis, smallest meaningful component, learning progression, evidence strength, and myths.
Lesson 4

Ideate and Prototype

Multiple product types, AI value decision, prompt design, Gem options, low-resolution prototypes, and safety.
Lesson 5

Test, Improve, Reflect, and Share

Staged testing, observation, revision, educational justification, presentation, and next-cycle decisions.

Lesson-to-project alignment

LessonStudent artifactResponsible AI and accessibility check
1Human-learning and AI principlesAnswer substitution, bias, privacy, fixed learning-style myth
2Empathy findingsPermission, privacy, non-leading questions, respectful representation
3Problem, task map, progression, evidence recordSource verification, hallucinations, learner-deficit assumptions
4Concept comparison and low-resolution prototypeData minimization, agency, access, alternatives, tool risk
5Testing record, revision, reflection, presentationConsent, stopping, unexpected harm, permissions, honest limitations
Student AI Learning Innovation League

A human-centered learning-design program in which students investigate authentic learning challenges, use learning science, create and test learning tools, and improve them through evidence and feedback.
