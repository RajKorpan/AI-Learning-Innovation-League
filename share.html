Share

Present what you learned and how the design changed.

A strong presentation explains the problem, learning strategy, AI behavior, test evidence, revision, safeguards, and limitations.

  • Learning goal and learner need
  • Specific challenge and task analysis
  • Learning strategy and design rationale
  • Prototype demonstration
  • Testing evidence and revisions
  • Safety, privacy, accessibility, and limitations
Presentation structure

A five-minute project story

  1. Introduce the learner and learning goal without revealing private information.
  2. Explain the specific challenge and evidence.
  3. Describe the learning strategy and why it fits.
  4. Demonstrate the AI interaction.
  5. Share testing evidence, revisions, safeguards, and limitations.
