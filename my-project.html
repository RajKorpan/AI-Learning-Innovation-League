My Project

Track your learning-aid design.

Use the project process to record your goal, challenge, strategy, prompt, testing evidence, revisions, and final reflection.

  1. Define the learning goal.
  2. Identify the specific challenge.
  3. Analyze the task.
  4. Select a strategy.
  5. Design and build.
  6. Test and revise.
  7. Share your evidence and limitations.

Recommended design notebook entries

  • Initial idea and intended learner
  • Interview or observation evidence
  • Task analysis
  • Strategy decision and rationale
  • Prompt or storyboard versions
  • Safety review
  • User-test notes
  • Revision decisions
  • Final reflection and limitations
  • Team contribution record
