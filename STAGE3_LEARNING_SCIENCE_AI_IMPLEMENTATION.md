# Stage 3 — Learning Science + AI Workflow Implementation

## Recommendation checklist

- **28 — Just-in-time learning science:** Implemented. The strategy stage teaches through a warm-up game, matcher, selected-strategy mini-lab, and only then reveals the relevant learning-science term and explanation.
- **29 — Student introduction before Strategy Explorer:** Implemented as the Strategy Warm-up before the matcher.
- **30 — Game/activity instead of text explanation:** Implemented as four interactive “Pick the move” rounds with immediate feedback and retry.
- **31 — Plain language first, formal terms second:** Implemented throughout Strategy Explorer and the selected-strategy mini-lab. Formal names are in expandable Learn More content.
- **32 — Only needed learning science:** Implemented. Students no longer receive a front-loaded catalog; detailed explanations appear only for top matches and the selected strategy.
- **33 — Learning science connected to product:** Implemented. Every strategy result states what the learner does, what the product does, and what success looks like.
- **34 — Narrow AI content:** Implemented as five design essentials in Prototype rather than a broad AI-literacy unit.
- **35 — Short, useful AI explanations:** Implemented as one-sentence design essentials tied directly to building.
- **36 — AI supports rather than replaces thinking:** Implemented as a repeated League design principle in Prototype, Design Check, Prompt Builder, Test, and facilitator guidance.
- **37 — Prompt Builder rationale:** Verified and strengthened. Prototype includes the learner → goal → breakdown → strategy → AI role → feedback → tool visual plus vague-vs-designed prompt comparison.
- **38 — Prompting progression:** Verified. First prompt → compare → guided design → Draft 1 → Test 1 → revision → Draft 2 → Test 2 remains built into the wizard.
- **39 — More completed prompt/Gem examples:** Expanded from three to four curated examples, including a hybrid hands-on reflection coach. Each now shows learner role, AI role, design rationale, and full prompt.
- **40 — Broader build options:** Verified and strengthened. Prototype prominently shows physical, printable/game, conversational AI, hybrid, and advanced digital possibilities.
- **41 — Off-screen/low-tech prominent:** Implemented as a dedicated off-screen callout with cards, paper, objects, role-play, movement, and partner activities.

## Facilitator alignment
Session 3 now tells facilitators to teach learning science after students choose a strategy rather than front-loading it. Session 4 includes the five AI design essentials and a prompt-design sequence. Session 5 explicitly asks who did the important thinking during AI-supported testing.
