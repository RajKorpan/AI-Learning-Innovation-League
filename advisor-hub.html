Advisor priorities

Coach the process without taking over.

Protect student ownership

Ask questions, provide feedback, and model tools, but keep the core decisions, design, and explanation in students’ hands.

Review safety and policy

Confirm approved platforms, privacy requirements, age restrictions, school policy, accessibility, and human oversight before testing.

Document implementation

Record what was taught, what students produced, what barriers emerged, and what should be revised for the next cycle.

Quick start

Suggested first actions

  1. Choose the club format and age range.
  2. Review school AI policies, device access, and platform restrictions.
  3. Recruit students and communicate with families.
  4. Prepare the five introductory lessons.
  5. Set up a project schedule and advisor checkpoints.
  6. Plan how students will test and share their tools.
Advisor workflow

A recommended sequence for implementation

  1. Complete the school-readiness review and identify an approved implementation format.
  2. Review the five lessons, student tool-creation process, and project rubric.
  3. Recruit students and communicate expectations to families.
  4. Teach the introductory lessons and collect baseline feedback.
  5. Guide students through project checkpoints without taking over design decisions.
  6. Approve safety and user-testing plans before students involve others.
  7. Collect teacher, student, family, and usability feedback.
  8. Prepare a local showcase and document revisions for the next cycle.
Common implementation questions

What advisors should decide early

How long will the club run?

Select a six-session, ten-week, or semester roadmap based on available time and student needs.

What technology is realistic?

Choose among no-code tools, general AI assistants, advisor-mediated tools, or paper prototypes.

How will students work?

Decide whether projects are individual, paired, team-based, mixed-age, or one-on-one.

How will success be documented?

Use pre/post measures, project rubrics, testing logs, implementation notes, and showcase artifacts.
