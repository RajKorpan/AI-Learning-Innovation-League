Printable Facilitation Guide

Session 4 · Prototype

Help students choose a build path and make the smallest version another person can actually try.

Back to guide

Key question: What is the least we can build to learn whether this idea helps?

Prepare

  • Have the focused challenge, selected strategy, and preferred direction available.
  • Preview the three prototype paths and Build Plan template.
  • Confirm which AI tools students may use.
  • Gather quick-build materials and make sure teams are not blocked by inaccessible accounts or devices.

Watch for

  • Polishing before testing the core interaction.
  • Building more than is needed for one person to try.
  • AI doing thinking the learner should be doing.
  • Technical complexity being treated as evidence of quality.

If students get stuck, try asking

  • What is one question this prototype should help you answer?
  • What is the smallest version that could answer it?
  • What important thinking must the learner still do?
  • Is AI helping in a way that actually adds value?

Quick check

  • Is there an actual prototype another person can try?
  • Does the learner still do the important thinking?
  • Is AI serving a clear purpose in the product or build process?
  • Could the team test the key interaction without building more?
