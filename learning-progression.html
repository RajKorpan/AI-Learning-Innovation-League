Learning-Progression Mapping Tool

Place the smallest component inside a larger disciplinary pathway.

A learning tool should not treat a skill as isolated. Teams map what comes before, what the skill enables, what is often confused with it, and how performance develops.

Responsible AI and accessibility checkpoint

Before leaving the learning-progression stage

  • Do not treat the progression as fixed for every learner.
  • Use teacher-client knowledge and credible disciplinary sources.
  • Distinguish missing instruction from learner deficit.
  • Include accessible ways to demonstrate performance.
  • Check that the selected component still connects to the teacher-client need.
Student AI Learning Innovation League

A human-centered learning-design program in which students investigate authentic learning challenges, use learning science, create and test learning tools, and improve them through evidence and feedback.
