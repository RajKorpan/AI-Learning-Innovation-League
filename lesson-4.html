Five-Lesson Curriculum

Lesson 4: Design an AI-Supported Learning Aid

Essential question: How can we instruct an AI system to support learning responsibly?

Lesson 1 Lesson 2 Lesson 3 Lesson 4 Lesson 5
Learning goals
  • Define the role, audience, goal, process, and limits of the AI interaction.
  • Write or revise structured instructions for a no-code tool or chatbot.
  • Include accuracy, privacy, safety, and human oversight.
Suggested outputs
  • Prompt builder
  • Prototype planning
  • Safety checklist
  • Build or storyboard

Learning-science concepts

ScaffoldingFeedbackGradual releaseLearner agency

AI-literacy concepts

Prompt structureContext and constraintsHallucinationsBiasPrivacyResponsible use
Suggested lesson flow
  1. Launch the essential question with a concrete example.
  2. Introduce the core learning-science and AI-literacy ideas.
  3. Engage students in an active sort, model, interview, design task, or user test.
  4. Create a student artifact that becomes part of the final project.
  5. Use an exit reflection or advisor checkpoint to prepare for the next lesson.
Adaptation note: This lesson can be taught in a classroom, small group, virtual club, or one-on-one tutoring setting. Adjust examples, reading level, and discussion structure for the age group.
Prompt anatomy

Required design elements

Role and audience

Who is the AI helping, and what kind of coach or partner should it be?

Goal and challenge

What should the learner achieve, and where do they get stuck?

Interaction sequence

What should happen first, next, after an error, and at the end?

Feedback

How should the AI identify strengths, gaps, and next steps?

Limits

What must the AI avoid doing?

Safeguards

How will privacy, accuracy, fairness, and uncertainty be handled?
