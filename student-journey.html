Student Journey

Use design thinking to understand, create, test, and improve.

The five standard design-thinking modes—Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—are connected, iterative modes. The League adds student-friendly actions and a learning-science evidence check.

EmpathizeUnderstand + Listen
DefineDiagnose + Research
IdeateImagine
PrototypeBuild
TestTest + Improve
Iteration rule: Testing is not the end. When evidence shows that the problem, strategy, or tool is wrong, return to an earlier mode.

UnderstandHow do human learning and AI differ?Learning and AI concept notes
ListenWhat do teachers and learners experience?Empathy interview notes

ResearchHow does this component develop?Evidence record

TestWhat do users actually do?Observation and learning evidence
ImproveWhat must change?Revision log
ShareWhy is the design educationally justified?Presentation and reflection
Student AI Learning Innovation League

A human-centered learning-design program in which students investigate authentic learning challenges, use learning science, create and test learning tools, and improve them through evidence and feedback.
