Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Do students need coding experience?

No. Students may use no-code tools, adapt an existing example, or create a detailed storyboard or prompt prototype.

Does every club need to hold a competition?

No. A club may begin with lessons, prototypes, testing, and a local sharing event. Competitions are part of the longer-term League vision.

Can the curriculum be used one-on-one?

Yes. Advisors can adapt peer interviews and user testing through reflection, teacher input, family feedback, or virtual collaboration.

What if our school blocks AI tools?

Students can use paper prototypes, scripted conversation flows, or approved local tools while advisors work through policy and access questions.

What should students create?

A responsible AI-supported learning aid grounded in a specific learning goal, diagnosed challenge, and evidence-based learning strategy.

How should student privacy be protected?

Do not enter unnecessary personal information, confirm school and platform rules, use de-identified examples, and review all testing plans before implementation.
