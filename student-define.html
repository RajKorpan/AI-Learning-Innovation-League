AI can suggest task steps or possible explanations, but it can be wrong. Trust what you saw, heard, and tested with the learner. Use outside sources only when you need to check an important claim.

Define check

Show your task map to your facilitator. Explain what the learner can already do, where it starts to get hard, what you saw, your learning goal, and what success would look like. Be ready to change your best guess.

Access check

Ask if access is part of the problem. Are the directions, reading, language, visuals, movement, pace, attention, or device getting in the way? Fix access first when you can.
