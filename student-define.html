About learning: an incorrect answer can be the final symptom of an earlier breakdown. Decompose the task so you can distinguish prerequisites, decisions, representations, and strategies.

About AI: AI can help suggest possible task steps or research terms, but its explanation can be incomplete or wrong. Your interviews, observations, work samples, and verified sources are the evidence.

Required checkpoint

Explain what the learner can already do, where the breakdown seems to happen, what evidence supports it, and what evidence could prove you wrong. Do not move on only because the tool produced a “ready” score.

Accessibility check

Could the apparent learning problem actually be an access problem—for example unclear directions, reading demand, language, visual presentation, motor interaction, pacing, attention, or device access? If so, redesign the environment or access first.
