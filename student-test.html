Learning Lab

About learning: enjoyment and usability matter, but they are not the same as learning. Watch for evidence that the target knowledge, skill, strategy, or reasoning changes—with less support when possible.

About AI: AI behavior can vary across prompts and attempts. Test edge cases, wrong answers, requests for shortcuts, and situations where the tool should admit uncertainty.

★
Example in action · Plan and observe

Test plan

Ask the learner to compare two unfamiliar lines, explain which is steeper, then measure rise/run. Observe whether the visual explanation changes.

League signature check

Did the learner do the important thinking—or did AI do it for them?

If AI is part of the prototype, test the division of work. The learner should still have to retrieve, explain, compare, decide, make, monitor, or reflect. Fast completion is not automatically learning.

AI can prompt→Learner thinks→AI responds to the attempt→Learner tries again

For a non-AI prototype, use the same question more broadly: did the tool make the learner think, or did it make the task easier by doing the thinking for them?

Step 4

Choose the lightest useful test

Use the lightest test that answers your question

1

Team self-test

Catch broken instructions, missing pieces, and obvious technical problems.

2

Peer test

Check whether another person understands what to do. Do not treat peer success as evidence for a different target learner.

3

Teacher review

Check subject alignment, realism, misconceptions, and whether the target problem still matters.

4

Learner test

When approved, observe the intended learner doing a realistic task and compare the target behavior.

Student + advisor

Testing checkpoint

Before testing with anyone outside the team, confirm the participant, task, permissions, privacy, stopping rule, accessibility, evidence target, and what claims your test can and cannot support.

Accessibility check

Who might have difficulty starting or continuing? Did the tester need help understanding what to do? Can the learner pause, use another format, control pacing, and recover from an error without unnecessary frustration?

Example in action · Use evidence to revise

Revision

Separate direction from steepness and add a comparison that includes positive and negative lines before retesting.
