Stage flow

Test the learning interaction—not whether someone likes the idea

1

Choose the test question

Decide what you need to learn from this version before anyone uses it.

2

Choose the right tester

Use a realistic learner or another appropriate reviewer and follow advisor guidance for permission and privacy.

3

Observe before explaining

Watch what the person does, where they hesitate, and what they use successfully.

4

Revise because of evidence

Change the tool because of something you learned, then test the revised version.

Required checkpoint

Before testing with a learner, confirm the participant, task, permissions, evidence target, accessibility, and stopping rule with your advisor.

Accessibility check

Who might have difficulty using this version? Did the learner need help understanding what to do? Was the barrier caused by the learning challenge or by the design itself?
