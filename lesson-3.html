Five-Lesson Curriculum

Lesson 3: Match the Challenge to a Learning Strategy

Essential question: What kind of support would actually help?

Lesson 1 Lesson 2 Lesson 3 Lesson 4 Lesson 5
Learning goals
  • Select a learning strategy that matches the diagnosed challenge.
  • Explain why the strategy should help.
  • Describe what the learner must still do during the AI interaction.
Suggested outputs
  • Strategy matching activity
  • Case analysis
  • Strategy rationale
  • Advisor checkpoint

Learning-science concepts

Retrieval practiceElaborationWorked examplesFeedbackChunkingSelf-explanation

AI-literacy concepts

Designing AI around a strategyPrompting for learningAvoiding generic answer-giving
Suggested lesson flow
  1. Launch the essential question with a concrete example.
  2. Introduce the core learning-science and AI-literacy ideas.
  3. Engage students in an active sort, model, interview, design task, or user test.
  4. Create a student artifact that becomes part of the final project.
  5. Use an exit reflection or advisor checkpoint to prepare for the next lesson.
Adaptation note: This lesson can be taught in a classroom, small group, virtual club, or one-on-one tutoring setting. Adjust examples, reading level, and discussion structure for the age group.
