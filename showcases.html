Showcases

Share the learning, evidence, and design story.

A showcase should make student thinking visible—not simply demonstrate a chatbot.

Required explanation

Learning goal, specific challenge, task analysis, selected strategy, and AI interaction.

Evidence

User-test observations, revisions, safety review, and remaining limitations.

Recognition

Learning-science design, responsible AI, accessibility, feedback, revision, teamwork, and community impact.

Suggested showcase stations

Make the event interactive

Project demonstration

Visitors try a short interaction and give feedback.

Design notebook

Students show evidence, prompt versions, testing notes, and revisions.

Judge interview

Students answer questions about the learning challenge, strategy, safeguards, and limitations.

Peer critique

Teams identify one strength and one evidence-based revision.

Family feedback

Families comment on clarity, usefulness, trust, and accessibility.

Reflection wall

Students share what they learned about AI and human learning.
