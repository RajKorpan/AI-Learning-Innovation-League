What the League is

A human-centered learning-design program

Starts with real needs

Students begin with learning problems described by teachers and peers—not with an app idea.

Uses learning science

Teams diagnose the smallest meaningful component and connect it to credible evidence about learning.

Designs for other learners

Students create one shared team product for a teacher-client and intended learner.

Uses many tool types

The solution may be paper, a game, flashcards, a graphic organizer, a learning activity, a prompt, a Gem, a chatbot, or a custom application.

Tests in the real world

Teams observe what users actually do and revise when evidence challenges their assumptions.

Combines curriculum, clubs, and showcases

The innovation is the League model—not a single AI platform.

The role of AI

AI is a resource—not the goal

Research partner

Help generate questions, search terms, candidate sources, and alternative explanations. Important claims must be verified.

Design assistant

Support brainstorming, scenarios, examples, critique, and wording without making the final educational decision.

Tool-building platform

Support a Gem, chatbot, or application when conversational or generative behavior adds genuine value.

Delivery mechanism

Guide practice, role-play, reflection, retrieval, feedback, or simulation within a carefully designed interaction.

Critical object of study

Students examine hallucinations, bias, privacy, answer substitution, and human-versus-machine reasoning.

Optional

The best solution may not use AI at all.
