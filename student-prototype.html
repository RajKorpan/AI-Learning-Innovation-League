SELF-CHECK

Does the prototype preserve the learning?

  • It addresses the Define learning challenge.
  • The learner still does the important thinking.
  • The selected learning strategy is visible in the interaction.
  • You can observe whether the learning action happens.
  • The prototype is accessible enough to test.
  • Important AI outputs have been checked.
  • It is small enough to change quickly.

Access question: Can the planned learner understand and use this prototype without unnecessary barriers?

Check the prototype together

  • Is there an actual prototype another person can try?
  • Does it target the learning challenge?
  • Is the learning interaction observable?
  • Is it safe, appropriate, and accessible enough to test?
