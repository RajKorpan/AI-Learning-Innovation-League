Team Management

Support productive, inclusive student teams.

Use roles, routines, checkpoints, and flexible grouping to help students participate meaningfully.

Suggested roles

Peer interviewer, task analyst, learning-strategy lead, AI designer, safety reviewer, tester, documenter, and presenter.

Group routines

Begin with goals, assign responsibilities, use short progress checks, rotate roles, and end with reflection.

Inclusive participation

Offer individual, pair, mixed-age, virtual, and one-on-one formats; provide multiple ways to communicate and contribute.

Common team challenges

Practical advisor responses

ChallengeAdvisor response
One student dominates technical workRotate roles and require each student to explain the learning rationale.
Students cannot agree on a projectUse evidence from learner interviews and the rubric to compare options.
Technology experience differs widelySeparate learning-design roles from platform-building roles and use paired work.
A team falls behindReduce scope to one learner, one challenge, one strategy, and one test case.
Participation is unevenUse contribution logs, short check-ins, and individually assigned deliverables.
