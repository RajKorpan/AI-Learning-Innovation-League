Learning-Design Prompt Builder

Generate instructions for more than a conventional tutor.

The builder translates empathy findings, task analysis, learning-science evidence, productive struggle, feedback, fading, reflection, motivation, accessibility, and measurement into a structured prompt.

Generated instructions

Generated-tool behavior

  • Ask before telling.
  • Diagnose the learner’s thinking.
  • Encourage explanation and evidence.
  • Preserve productive struggle.
  • Provide graduated hints.
  • Offer feedback without completing the task.
  • Prompt reflection and strategy selection.
  • Check understanding and adapt to the response.
  • Avoid unsupported claims about fixed learning styles.
  • State limitations and encourage verification.
Responsible AI and accessibility checkpoint

Before leaving the prompt-design stage

  • Use non-identifying learner descriptions.
  • Require age-appropriate and respectful language.
  • Add refusal and escalation rules.
  • Make uncertainty visible.
  • Include accessible formats and low-tech alternatives.
  • Test for answer substitution, hallucinations, bias, and frustration.
Student AI Learning Innovation League

A human-centered learning-design program in which students investigate authentic learning challenges, use learning science, create and test learning tools, and improve them through evidence and feedback.
