Automated Prompt Builder

Turn a learning design into a complete AI instruction set.

The builder updates automatically as you enter the learner, goal, challenge, strategy, interaction steps, feedback rules, and safeguards.

Generated prompt
Quality check: The prompt should make the learner think, use the selected learning strategy, provide limited and useful support, protect privacy, and acknowledge uncertainty.
Prompt evaluation

Review the generated instructions before testing

  • The intended learner and learning goal are specific.
  • The challenge is defined without private or diagnostic information.
  • The interaction requires the learner to explain, practice, decide, or reflect.
  • The AI waits for the learner rather than producing a complete solution.
  • Feedback rules are specific and age appropriate.
  • The prompt explains how to handle uncertainty and important factual claims.
  • The design includes privacy, fairness, accessibility, and human-review safeguards.
