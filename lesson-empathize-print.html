Printable Facilitation Guide

Session 1 · Empathize

Help students learn from a real learner or teacher, record useful evidence, and resist jumping to a solution too soon.

Back to guide

Key question: What is the learner trying to do, and what seems to make it hard?

Prepare

  • Know who students will interview and confirm participation is appropriate.
  • Preview the student interview-planning section and the downloadable notes template.
  • Have the Empathy Map template available on paper or as a PDF.
  • Prepare a non-sensitive alternative for students who should not discuss a personal learning experience.

Watch for

  • Leading questions that suggest an answer or cause.
  • Students treating the quick build as their real solution.
  • AI practice being treated as evidence about the learner.
  • Recording identifying or sensitive information that is not needed.

If students get stuck, try asking

  • What did you actually hear or see?
  • Which part is evidence, and which part is still your best guess?
  • What is one thing you are still unsure about?

Plan the interview

1 × 45 min9–16

Students choose who they are learning from, select a STEM situation, and choose about four starter questions. Optional AI practice happens here, before the real interview.

1 × 45 min16–28

Check together

1 × 45 min38–45
2 × 45 minMeeting 2 · 30–45

Students discuss the map with a facilitator or partner and identify what is evidence, what is still a guess, and what needs more investigation.

Student activity: student-empathize.html#advisor-check

Quick check

  • Can students point to something they actually heard or observed?
  • Can they distinguish evidence from a best guess?
  • Do they still have at least one open question?
  • Are they still investigating rather than defending a solution?
