Family and Community Guide

Families help build the League—not only attend the final event.

Families contribute feedback on transparency, safety, privacy, accessibility, value, student support, and event experience. Community members who will use or be affected by the product should help shape it.

What students will experience

  • Study human learning, AI, and common misconceptions.
  • Interview a teacher-client and peer learners.
  • Analyze a specific learning challenge.
  • Research credible learning-science evidence.
  • Create one team learning tool that may or may not use AI.
  • Test, revise, reflect, and participate in a showcase.
  • Possibly complete approved surveys, interviews, or focus groups during the pilot.

How AI will and will not be used

AI mayAI will not be treated as
Support research, brainstorming, drafting, practice, feedback, simulation, or reflection.An automatic authority on learning, teaching, disability, placement, or student ability.
Help build a Gem, chatbot, or application when approved.A replacement for teachers, families, or learner thinking.
Be studied critically for errors, bias, privacy, and limitations.A requirement for every final product.

Family roles

Review communication

Comment on clarity, expectations, privacy, accommodations, and responsible AI.

Share learning experiences

Describe supports, barriers, routines, and questions without taking over the student solution.

Test a prototype

Provide feedback on clarity, usefulness, trust, and accessibility when appropriate.

Attend or support a showcase

Ask about evidence, learner agency, limitations, and next steps.

Complete a feedback survey

Help the team improve family communication, safety, logistics, and value.

Raise concerns

Use the project contact for privacy, accessibility, participation, or event concerns.

Events and logistics

Event dates, food, transportation, location, virtual access, interpretation, accessibility, and other logistics are TBD. Confirmed information should be communicated in advance, including whether food is provided and how dietary or access needs will be addressed.
