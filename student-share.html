There is more than one good ending.

You might share a new insight, a clearly defined problem, an early prototype, evidence from one test, or a more developed tool. Progress counts when you can explain what you learned and what you would do next.

If you used AI, explain what it helped with, what it could not know by itself, and what you checked. If you did not use AI in the final experience, explain why.

Your facilitator is the adult guiding your team. They help you think through important choices without doing the project for you.

You do not have to demo a finished tool. Pick one thing that helps the audience understand your progress.

1A learning clue

Show an Empathy Map clue, work sample, or task breakdown that changed your thinking.

2An early prototype

3A test

Step 5

See ways to share your work

Many kinds of progress are worth sharing

Choose what your team is ready to share

A League share-out is about making your thinking visible. A polished final product is not required.

💡 What we learnedA useful insight about the learner, human learning, AI, or the design process.
🎯 The problem we identifiedA clearer learning challenge or task breakdown that changed how you understand the problem.
🧰 An early prototypeA rough paper, physical, activity-based, or digital version and the question it is helping you investigate.
🧪 A testWhat a learner did, what the team noticed, and the revision or next experiment that followed.
✨ A more developed toolA tool or learning experience that has gone through more building and testing. It still does not have to be “done.”
The format can vary.

Your group might use a Learning Showcase, club share-out, classroom conversation, gallery walk, peer review, demonstration, or another facilitator-approved format. The exact venue and date are handled separately from this website.

What matters: explain what you know now, show something that supports the story, and name what you would investigate or improve next.

Step 6

Check together

Facilitator checkPause here for facilitator review or approval before moving on.
Do together

Before you share

Review your story, what you plan to show, privacy, access, backup plan, and speaking roles with your facilitator. Use the same check whether you are sharing in a showcase, club meeting, classroom, gallery walk, or another approved format.

Access check

Make sure people can follow your demo. Use clear visuals and speech. Add captions or text when needed. Bring a backup that does not need one device or internet.
