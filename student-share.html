Project story

The team explains that the learner could calculate slope but did not connect the ratio to visible steepness. Testing revealed a separate issue with negative direction.

Prepare a focused demonstration

1Set the challenge

Give enough context to understand the learning problem without identifying the learner.

2Show one meaningful interaction

Use a prepared example that reveals the learning strategy and the learner's role.

3Explain the evidence

Say what testing showed, what it did not show, and what you changed.

4Explain AI honestly

If AI was used, explain its role, limits, and what you verified. If it was not used, explain why another form fit better.

5Have a backup

Use screenshots, a recorded interaction, paper prototype, or storyboard if the device or internet fails.

6Invite a useful question

Be ready to discuss the learning problem, design choices, testing, limitations, and next step.

Evidence-sized claim

Accessibility check

Can the audience access the explanation and demonstration? Prepare readable visuals, clear spoken explanations, captions or transcripts when relevant, alternate formats, and a backup that does not depend on one device or internet connection.
