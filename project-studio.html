How to use the Studio

  1. Check whether the learning problem is the right size.
  2. Analyze the knowledge and actions required.
  3. Record learning-science claims and sources.
  4. Map the selected component in a larger progression.
  5. Compare learning strategies.
  6. Build a structured prompt when conversational AI fits.
  7. Reflect after every stage.
Privacy: Do not enter names, grades, medical information, account details, unapproved student work, or other identifying information into these tools.
