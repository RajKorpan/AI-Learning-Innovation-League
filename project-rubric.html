Student Project Rubric

Evaluate the learning design, not just the technology.

Use the rubric before, during, and after the project so expectations are clear.

Criterion4 — Advanced3 — Proficient2 — Developing1 — Beginning
Learning goalPrecise, observable, and focused on knowledge, reasoning, or skill.Clear and generally observable.Broad or partly task-focused.Vague or missing.
Specific challengePrecise and supported by evidence.Specific with some evidence.General difficulty.Broad label or assumption.
Task analysisThoroughly identifies knowledge, procedures, decisions, and reasoning.Identifies major components.Partial analysis.Minimal or inaccurate.
Learning strategyWell matched and clearly justified.Appropriate and explained.Possibly useful but weakly justified.Generic or mismatched.
AI interactionClearly aligned, preserves thinking, and gives useful feedback.Generally aligned and usable.Partly aligned or inconsistent.Mainly answer-focused.
Safety and privacySpecific protections for data, accuracy, fairness, and oversight.Appropriate core protections.General safety statements.Limited or missing.
TestingSystematic test with detailed evidence.Meaningful test and feedback.Limited testing.Informal or absent.
RevisionMeaningful changes directly tied to evidence.Appropriate evidence-based revision.Weak connection to evidence.Superficial or absent.
CommunicationExplains goal, challenge, strategy, AI, evidence, revisions, and limits.Explains most decisions clearly.Some connections are unclear.Focuses mostly on technology.
