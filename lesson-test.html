Session 5 · Test + Share

Help students run one useful test, make one evidence-based change, and prepare an honest share-out of what they learned.

Key questionWhat did the learner actually do, and what will we change because of it?
Share progress, not polish.Teams can share what they learned about the problem, an early prototype, a test and revision, or a more developed tool—as long as their claims match their evidence.

Before the session

10–15 min

Prepare

  • Have the learning challenge, success sign, current prototype, and Test Sheet available.
  • Confirm who may test and what privacy or participation boundaries apply.
  • Have the Share-out Planner available for the second half of the work.
  • Prepare an accessible backup way to show the project.

Watch for

  • Treating “they liked it” or “they finished” as proof of learning.
  • Leading the tester or rescuing them too quickly.
  • Making a revision that is not connected to evidence.
  • Share-out claims that go beyond what students actually observed.

If students get stuck, try asking

  • What would the learner do differently if this actually helped?
  • What did you see, hear, or measure?
  • What is one change you can make because of this test?
  • What can you claim—and what is still uncertain?

Quick check

  • Did students run at least one meaningful test?
  • Did at least one design change come from evidence?
  • Do share-out claims match the evidence?
  • Can students name what is still uncertain and what they would do next?
  • Do they have an accessible way to show the work?
