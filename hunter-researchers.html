Hunter College Researchers

Learning science, AI, computing education, and human-centered design.

The League brings together researchers, educators, students, and families to design a practical model for student-led AI innovation in STEM learning.

Hunter College connection

Research translated into student opportunity

The League is designed to connect school-based participation with Hunter College learning, research, mentoring, and public-engagement opportunities. The current planning phase focuses on a usable curriculum and club model; the longer-term pathway can expand lab connections, student-researcher interviews, mentoring, campus events, and advanced project opportunities.

Explore the Hunter College pathway
Ways researchers may engage

Translate research into student-facing experiences

Short interviews

Researchers explain what they study, how they ask questions, and how students can prepare for related fields.

Project critiques

Researchers ask students about evidence, assumptions, safeguards, and limitations.

Demonstrations

Labs share examples of trustworthy AI, robotics, data literacy, or human-centered design.

Near-peer mentoring

Undergraduates help students navigate tools, research skills, and college pathways.

Campus experiences

Students visit classrooms, labs, or showcases and connect club work to higher education.

Program improvement

Researchers use feedback from students, families, and educators to refine the League model.
