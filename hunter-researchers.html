Project Team

An interdisciplinary team across AI, learning science, educational technology, computing education, school implementation, and family engagement.

The planning team combines Hunter College researchers and technologists with a K–12 educator partner and a parent–educator partner.

RK

Raj Korpan

Principal Investigator · Hunter College Computer Science · Director, TIER Lab

Leads project planning, AI systems, computing education, technical evaluation, responsible AI, and trustworthy and explainable technology connections.

RB

Rhonda Bondie

Co-Principal Investigator · Director, Hunter College Learning Lab

Leads learning science, inclusive instructional design, curriculum, teacher implementation, evaluation planning, and HCLL pilot coordination.

KH

Kenny Hirschmann

Co-Principal Investigator and Technologist · Frankfort Center

Contributes instructional design, educational technology, advisor supports, implementation guidance, website and wireframe design, usability, and accessibility.

ET

Edgar Troudt

Co-Principal Investigator, Technologist, and Entrepreneur Lead · Computer Science Education

Supports the club model, AI for learning, computer science education, student tool-building, Lean Launch, sustainability, and teacher-education pathways.

LC

Luis Collado

K–12 Educator Partner · Brooklyn Technical High School

Contributes computer science classroom, robotics mentoring, student engagement, afterschool implementation, and feasibility expertise.

KH

Khalya Hopkins, Ed.D.

Parent and Guardian Partner · Educator and Researcher

Contributes family and caregiver perspectives, student support, achievement-oriented teaching practices, communication, transparency, privacy, accessibility, and community trust.
