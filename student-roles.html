Student Roles and Collaboration

Use different strengths to build one strong project.

Team members may hold one role or rotate across roles. Everyone should understand the learning goal, design, evidence, safeguards, and limitations.

Peer interviewer

Asks learners about goals, challenges, existing strengths, and helpful or unhelpful support.

Task analyst

Breaks the task into knowledge, procedures, decisions, reasoning, and checking.

Learning-strategy lead

Finds and justifies a strategy that matches the diagnosed challenge.

AI interaction designer

Defines the AI role, sequence, feedback, hints, limits, and learner responsibilities.

Safety and privacy reviewer

Checks data use, accuracy, bias, accessibility, school policy, and human oversight.

User-testing lead

Prepares the test, observes the user, records evidence, and organizes feedback.

Documentation lead

Maintains the design notebook, versions, decisions, evidence, and contribution log.

Presentation lead

Organizes the final explanation while ensuring every team member can answer questions.

Team agreement

  • We will listen to the learner before designing.
  • We will explain and document our own decisions.
  • We will challenge ideas respectfully.
  • We will share work fairly and record contributions.
  • We will test safely and revise using evidence.
  • We will state what our tool cannot do.
Collaboration routine

Use a short team cycle every meeting

  1. State the meeting goal and assign roles.
  2. Review evidence or feedback from the previous session.
  3. Complete one project task.
  4. Invite critique from another team member.
  5. Record decisions and contributions.
  6. End with the next action and responsible person.
