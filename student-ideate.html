Turn what you have into activity possibilities Resource Inventor

Instead of asking AI for a generic learning activity, build a detailed prompt that tells it what someone is trying to learn, which learning strategy you want to explore, and what materials, people, spaces, and time you actually have.

This tool creates a prompt; it does not send anything to AI.

Review the prompt, then paste it into an advisor-approved AI system. Treat the AI response as ideas to critique and remix—not as the answer.

Strategy checkpoint

Bring your top strategies and ideas to your advisor. Explain why each could fit the mapped breakdown, what evidence supports it, and what you could test cheaply before choosing a final product.

Accessibility check

Can you generate at least one idea that works in another format or with fewer access demands? Consider language, reading, sensory access, motor interaction, pacing, device availability, and whether another person can participate.
