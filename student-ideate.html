Turn what you have into several activity possibilities Resource Inventor

Build a detailed prompt that combines the learning goal, learning strategy, materials, learner interests, real constraints, and accessibility needs. The AI is asked for multiple possibilities; your team chooses, changes, combines, or rejects them.

When your prompt is ready
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Open the AI tool your advisor or school permits.
  3. Paste it and ask for several different possibilities.
  4. Return here. Compare, combine, reject, or revise the ideas rather than accepting one automatically.

Strategy checkpoint

Bring your focused learning objective, top strategy hypotheses, Resource Inventor possibilities, and finalist ideas to your advisor. Explain why the strategy fits, what the learner would actually do, what evidence would show improvement, and which idea is realistic to prototype.

Accessibility check

Can you generate at least one idea that works in another format or with fewer access demands? Consider language, reading, sensory access, motor interaction, pacing, device availability, and whether another person can participate.
