Stage flow

Do not choose a chatbot just because it is easy to imagine

1

Explore learning strategies

Use the learning breakdown and evidence to identify several strategies that might address it.

2

Ask “What do we already have?”

Look at classroom materials, devices, spaces, notes, manipulatives, and people that could become part of a learning activity.

3

Make deliberately different ideas

Try one no-AI idea, one idea using existing resources, one social/activity idea, and one AI/digital idea when appropriate.

4

Compare before choosing

Look at learner fit, strategy fit, accessibility, feasibility, and what each idea lets you test.
