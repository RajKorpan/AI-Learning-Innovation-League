Expanded Strategy Matcher

Match the learner’s actual process to a learning strategy.

The matcher suggests possibilities. It does not diagnose a learner or replace teacher judgment, task analysis, learning-science research, or testing.

Responsible AI and accessibility checkpoint

Before leaving the strategy-selection stage

  • Do not select a strategy from a fixed learning-style label.
  • Use evidence about the task and learner process.
  • Consider accessibility and prior knowledge.
  • State when the strategy may not fit.
  • Plan how support will fade and how learning will be tested.
Student AI Learning Innovation League

A human-centered learning-design program in which students investigate authentic learning challenges, use learning science, create and test learning tools, and improve them through evidence and feedback.
