Interactive Strategy Matcher

Find learning strategies that match the challenge.

Answer the questions based on evidence about the learner. The matcher suggests strategies, but the student team must make and justify the final choice.

1. What is the main difficulty?
2. What can the learner already do?
3. What should the learner do more of?

How to use the result responsibly

  1. Compare the suggested strategies with evidence about the learner.
  2. Read the full strategy descriptions in the library.
  3. Choose one primary strategy and, if needed, one supporting strategy.
  4. Explain why the strategy fits the challenge.
  5. Test whether the AI actually behaves according to the strategy.
