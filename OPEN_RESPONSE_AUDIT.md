# Student Open-Response Audit

Stage 2 audited difficult open-response demands across the six student stages.

## Site-wide behavior

Every student stage loads `assets/js/open-response-scaffold.js`. For static textareas that do not already have a purpose-built helper, the script adds a collapsed "Need a starting point?" control with a sentence starter. Fields where uncertainty is legitimate can also expose an "I'm not sure yet" response.

## Stage-specific high-demand thinking

### Empathize
The student no longer has to invent interview methodology from scratch. Question Detective teaches the core principle, Interview Coach supplies a question bank and sentence starters, and the Empathy Map organizes notes into six concrete categories.

### Define
The Task Map makes the task visible one step at a time. The built-in Task Breakdown Helper supports students who cannot yet identify the likely bottleneck. The helper's output is explicitly a draft best guess, not a final explanation.

### Ideate
Learning-challenge categories are taught through examples and practice cases before the Strategy Explorer. Resource Inventor uses structured resource cards and one-question-at-a-time prompts.

### Prototype
The Guided Build Planner and Prompt Builder already reveal one decision at a time and provide explanations/examples for difficult fields.

### Test
The testing tool is a guided wizard rather than a large open form. It starts from the carried-forward learning goal and sign of success.

### Share
The sharing tool is a guided wizard that assembles the project story one piece at a time rather than asking for an entire presentation narrative at once.

## Principle used in the audit

The site should not provide maximum text. It should provide the strongest scaffold at the moment the student encounters the hardest thinking.
